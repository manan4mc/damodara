What you are responsible for

  • Setting up Damodara and its backup settings correctly.
  • Checking that your backups are actually created and usable.
  • Having enough local disk space and enough Google Drive storage. Uploads can fail if either runs out.
  • Testing your restore process before you need it.
  • Your server, your Google account, and the files in your backups.

Live backups are best-effort

Damodara makes a non-blocking live copy while your server is running. Files that are changing during the copy may not all represent exactly the same moment, so a backup can be inconsistent. A backup is not guaranteed to be usable until you have checked it.

Restoring

Restore backups only with the Minecraft server stopped. Do not restore files into a running server.

Retention

Local retention may delete older local ZIP archives based on your configured limits. Damodara does not delete anything from Google Drive. You manage those files yourself.
