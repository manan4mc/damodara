Privacy Policy

Published: October 28, 2026

Damodara is a backup plugin that runs on your own Minecraft Paper server. This page explains what it accesses, what it stores, and what it doesn't do. The developer does not operate any servers that receive your data.

What Damodara does with Google Drive

After the server owner connects a Google account, Damodara uploads completed backup archives to that account's Google Drive. Each archive has a timestamp in its name.

The only permission Damodara requests from Google is:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission is limited to files that the app creates. Damodara only uploads new archives. It does not list, download, overwrite, or delete anything in your Drive.

Damodara also doesn't remove old backups from Google Drive. Uploaded archives stay there until the account owner deletes them.

What is stored on your server

  • Backup archives. ZIP backups are saved on the server, by default in ./backups/. Damodara's local retention settings may delete older local archives. They never affect copies in Google Drive.
  • Google credentials. Your sign-in credentials are saved at plugins/Damodara/credentials/credentials.json. This file is plaintext and is not encrypted at rest. On systems that support it, Damodara tries to limit the file and its folder to the owner account. That doesn't protect the file from anyone who can access the server files as that same owner.

Backups contain whatever server files they include, which can be sensitive. Please keep your server and your Google account secure.

Who can access your data

Only you, and anyone you give access to your server files or your Google account. The developer does not receive your Google credentials or your backup files.

What Damodara doesn't do

  • It doesn't use analytics or telemetry.
  • It doesn't check for updates.
  • It doesn't contact any server run by the developer. Its network requests go straight to Google's sign-in and Drive services.

A note about live backups

Damodara copies files while your server is running, so backups are best-effort. Files that change during the copy may be captured at different moments.

Disconnecting Google Drive

To disconnect your Google account, run /damodara auth disconnect from the server console. To stop uploads, you can also set google-drive.enabled: false in the plugin configuration. Neither step removes archives that are already in Drive, so delete those yourself if you want them gone.

This website

This site is plain static pages. It has no scripts, analytics, trackers, or cookies.
